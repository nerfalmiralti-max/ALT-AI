# ALT QR visual contract

- Primary implementation: `app/globals.css`, `components/qr/home.tsx`, `scan-form.tsx`, and `scan-report.tsx`.
- Current strengths: graphite/off-white palette, condensed headlines, monospace labels, ruled issue register, registration motifs, measurable stage progress, and real evidence imagery.
- V2 hierarchy: release gate and ship verdict first; score and delta second; blocker/new/fixed counts third; page/network/console evidence and comparisons below.
- Status color is semantic: pass/ready, warning/attention, and blocker/failure. Never color ordinary decoration as status.
- Use native semantic controls, visible focus, at least 44 px touch targets where practical, and no horizontal document overflow at supported widths.
- Print: remove navigation/actions, avoid clipped details, force light paper/ink colors, preserve URLs and evidence labels, and use stable page breaks.
